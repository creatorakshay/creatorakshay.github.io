- **Goal:** Be the go-to for SAP + external AI discussions

- **Goal:** Tangible, external-facing artefacts

- **Goal:** Formal validation + public visibility
- **Key Outputs:** SAP BTP cert, Azure AI Engineer cert (optional), rebranded LinkedIn/CV
- **Checkpoint:** Already perceived as the target role

## Why This Plan Works

### ✅ Architect-First, Not Data-Scientist-First
Building on your existing SAP architecture expertise, not requiring a complete pivot. You stay in the architectural design space where your value compounds.

### ✅ Strong SAP Anchoring (Your Unfair Advantage)
Your deep SAP knowledge is your moat. Everyone doing "AI architect" is doing it generically. You're doing it for the world's #1 enterprise software platform.

### ✅ Continuous Visible Output
Every month produces tangible artefacts (diagrams, repos, posts, blog drafts). Visibility compounds over time and builds your brand.

### ✅ Clear Market Signal by Design
LinkedIn refresh, certifications, GitHub repos, and speaking all reinforce the same narrative: SAP + AI integration architect. No ambiguity.

## Notes

### What NOT to Do
- ❌ Deep dive into ML research (focus on applied patterns)
- ❌ Build production systems (focus on architectures & demos)
- ❌ Generic "AI architect" messaging (anchor to SAP always)
- ❌ Ignore governance (it's a key differentiator)

### Flexibility Points
- Can shift 1-2 weeks between months
- Stretch weeks are optional (aim for 2-3 total)
- Can combine topics if they overlap
- Can add external events/speaking as bonus

### Mindset
- You're becoming an **architect** who understands AI, not an AI expert doing SAP
- Your value is **integration** and **governance**, not cutting-edge research
- Your unfair advantage is **SAP domain knowledge**
- Your goal is **credibility and visibility** by December 1
